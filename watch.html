Synopsis

Whiskers sleeps through his alarm, stretches, winds up the gramophone and dances a happy rubber-hose routine. Squeak sneaks out of the mouse hole, snatches the record and runs. The chase ends in a gramophone crash, a dazed count of stars — and the discovery that the wrecked gramophone makes a fine banjo. Cat and mouse finish with a duet, confetti and a tip of the boater.
